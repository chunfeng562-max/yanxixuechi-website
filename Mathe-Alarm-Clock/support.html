Contact support

For faster troubleshooting, include your device model, iOS version, Math Alarm app version, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots are welcome, but please remove sensitive information first.

Email Math Alarm Support

Before contacting us

  1. Update Math Alarm and iOS to versions compatible with your device.
  2. Open Math Alarm once after installing or updating it.
  3. Confirm alarm and notification permissions are enabled in iOS Settings.
  4. Check the alarm’s time, repeat days, sound, and enabled status.
  5. Restart the App and your device, then create a short test alarm.

Safety reminder: Do not rely on any software alarm as your only alert for emergencies, medical needs, safety-critical events, travel, or other high-consequence situations.

Frequently asked questions

My alarm did not appear or make a sound. What should I check?
  • Confirm the alarm is enabled and its next scheduled date is correct.
  • In iOS Settings, allow Math Alarm to use alarms and notifications where those controls are available.
  • Check the device volume, sound output, Focus settings, and that the device is powered on.
  • Open Math Alarm after reinstalling or updating so it can schedule alarms again.
  • Create a test alarm a few minutes in the future. If it still fails, email support with your iOS and App versions.
Why is a Live Activity not visible?

Live Activities require a supported iPhone and iOS version, permission in iOS Settings, and an active alarm state that the App has started. Open the main App, confirm Live Activities are enabled for Math Alarm, and trigger a test alarm. Live Activity presentation differs by device and may appear on the Lock Screen even when Dynamic Island is unavailable.

Why did the math challenge not open automatically?

Some alarm and notification actions depend on iOS state and user interaction. Tap the Math Alarm alert or open the App to continue the challenge. If the App repeatedly fails to show the challenge after opening, contact support with reproduction steps.

Where is my alarm and progress data stored?

The current version stores alarm schedules, app preferences, and progress data locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not transmit this data to the developer. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual alarms or reset applicable settings in the App. Uninstalling Math Alarm removes the App and its local data from that device, subject to normal iOS behavior. Because the developer does not receive this local data, there is no remote account database to delete. See Privacy Choices.

Does Math Alarm track me or show ads?

No. The reviewed version contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDKs, or user accounts. If this changes in a future version, the Privacy Policy and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

What information should I avoid sending in a support email?

Do not send passwords, financial details, government identifiers, health records, or other sensitive personal information. Only include technical details needed to understand the issue.
