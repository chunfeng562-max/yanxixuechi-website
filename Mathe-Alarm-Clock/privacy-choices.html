Your controls

Privacy Choices

Math Alarm keeps functional App data on your device. These are the controls available to you.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

No online account: Math Alarm has no account system or developer-operated cloud database containing your alarms, app preferences, or progress data. You manage that data directly on your device.

Manage or delete local App data

  • Alarms: edit or delete alarms from the alarm list in Math Alarm.
  • Settings and preferences: change applicable preferences in the App’s Settings screen.
  • All local App data: uninstall Math Alarm using the standard iOS process. This removes the App and its local container from that device, subject to normal iOS behavior.

Because this information is not transmitted to us, we cannot remotely view, export, correct, or delete it.

Manage permissions

Open the iOS Settings app and locate Math Alarm to review controls made available by your iOS version. You may be able to manage:

  • Alarm access
  • Notifications, sounds, badges, and Lock Screen presentation
  • Live Activities
  • Background behavior controlled by iOS

Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding App feature from working. You can also review system-wide Focus, sound, and Live Activity settings.

Support email records

If you have emailed support, you may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information contained in that support conversation, subject to applicable legal exceptions. Send the request from the same email address used in the original conversation so we can reasonably verify it.

Choices that are not needed in the current version

The current version does not include behavioral advertising, cross-app tracking, sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, marketing mailing lists, or an online App account. Therefore, there is no advertising opt-out, tracking consent withdrawal, marketing unsubscribe, or cloud-account deletion process for the App.
