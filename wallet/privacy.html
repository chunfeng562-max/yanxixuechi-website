Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Wallet prelaunch website. The mobile app is still in development, and its complete privacy disclosures will be published before release.

Last updated and effective: August 19, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Wallet prelaunch webpages at yanxixuechi-energy.com/wallet/ (the “Website”). The Website is provided by Nanjing Yanxi Xuechi Energy Development Co., Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It does not yet govern a released Wallet mobile application.

2. Information we collect through this Website

Short version: The Website has no account system, document upload, payment form, advertising cookie, or analytics tag. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive files through the Website or by email.

We do not ask you to enter personal information into a web form. The Website uses ordinary links, including links that open your email application. We do not sell personal information or use Website information to track you across unrelated apps or websites.

3. Early-access and support email

If you choose an email link, your email application may prefill a message to us. When you send it, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, record product interest, provide launch updates you requested, and handle support questions.

Joining early access by email does not create an account, purchase, or binding reservation. You may ask us to stop sending launch updates at any time.

4. Website hosting and standard request data

Our hosting and network providers may automatically process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the Website, such as IP address, browser or device type, requested URL, date and time, and security or error logs. Their handling of this information is subject to their own policies and contractual obligations.

We do not place our own advertising or analytics cookies on these Wallet webpages. External links, your email provider, and other services you choose to use operate under their own privacy terms.

5. Retention

We retain early-access and support emails only as reasonably necessary to provide requested updates, answer questions, maintain appropriate business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We may retain a minimal suppression record after an opt-out so that we can respect the request.

6. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to send an email. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information in your correspondence, or opt out of launch updates, by contacting us. Applicable law may provide additional privacy rights. We may need to verify a request and may retain information where legally required or reasonably needed for security and dispute resolution.

7. Children’s privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information handled through the Website and email. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Do not email passwords, Social Security numbers, payment-card data, medical records, identity-document images, or other highly sensitive information.

9. Mobile app privacy

Wallet is still in development. Before release, we will publish a separate or updated privacy disclosure describing the app’s storage, permissions, security features, backups, sharing behavior, purchases, and any service providers. Statements on this prelaunch Website should not be read as final technical specifications for the unreleased app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Website, app, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date. We will provide additional notice where required by law.
