Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Premium?
Open 5M Sleep, go to Settings or a Premium screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Reinstalling the app does not require buying again while the verified subscription remains active.
How do I cancel or manage a subscription?
Open iOS Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, then select 5M Sleep. You can also use Apple’s subscription management page. Deleting 5M Sleep does not cancel a subscription.
How do I request a refund?
Apple handles App Store payments and refund decisions. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and select the relevant purchase. We cannot issue App Store refunds directly.
Why does a subscription appear temporarily unavailable?
Confirm that your device is online and signed into the App Store, then relaunch the app and try again. Product changes can also take time to become available in Apple’s storefront or Sandbox. If the issue continues, send us your device model, iOS version, storefront country or region, and a screenshot.
Why does audio stop when the screen locks?
Make sure audio is playing before locking the screen. Check the sleep timer and confirm your AirPods, Bluetooth, or AirPlay output is still connected. If needed, force quit 5M Sleep, reopen it, and start playback again.
How does the sleep timer work?
Choose a timer duration while audio is playing. At the end of the timer, 5M Sleep fades audio out and stops playback. A timer set to a short duration is a common reason for unexpected stopping.
Where are my favorites and recent sounds stored?
Favorites, recent items, onboarding status, and basic settings are stored locally on your device. 5M Sleep does not require an account and does not upload your listening history to our servers.
Is 5M Sleep a medical or sleep-treatment app?
No. 5M Sleep is a wellness and audio app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat sleep or health conditions. Contact a qualified healthcare professional about persistent sleep problems or hearing concerns.
