Privacy

Your privacy choices

Last updated: July 27, 2026

5M Sleep has no user accounts and does not send us your listening history or in-app preferences.

Delete information stored by the App

Favorites, recent items, onboarding status, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting 5M Sleep removes this on-device information. You can also change favorites and preferences directly in the App.

Manage purchase information

Apple controls App Store purchase history and active subscriptions. Manage an active subscription through Apple’s subscription settings. For Apple privacy requests, use privacy.apple.com.

Request access, correction, or deletion

If you have contacted support, you may ask us to access, correct, or delete the support information we hold. Email support@tower-energy.store with the subject “5M Sleep Privacy Request.”

To protect you, we may need to verify that the request comes from the email address involved. We cannot identify or provide on-device app information because the App does not transmit it to us.

Tracking and advertising choices

5M Sleep does not contain advertising SDKs, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. There is therefore no in-app advertising or tracking opt-out to configure.
