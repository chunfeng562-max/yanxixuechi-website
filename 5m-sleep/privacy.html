1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the 5M Sleep mobile application and its support and legal webpages (collectively, the “Service”). The Service is provided by Tower Energy, a product of Nanjing Yanxi Xuechi Energy Development Co., Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

2. Information we collect

Short version: The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

We do not collect through the app your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, health data, microphone recordings, or advertising identifier.

We do not use information from the app to track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

5M Sleep stores limited information locally so the app can remember your choices, including:

  • Favorites and recently played items;
  • Onboarding completion and answers used to prepare an initial sound mix;
  • Playback, timer, and interface preferences; and
  • Local counters used to decide when an in-app rating invitation may be appropriate.

This information is not uploaded to our servers. Audio is played from resources included with the app; your mixes and listening history are not uploaded.

4. Apple, purchases, and subscriptions

Purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your full Apple Account credentials or payment-card details. The app receives limited product and transaction information from Apple, such as product identifier, purchase status, and expiration date, only as needed to display offers, verify entitlement, restore purchases, and unlock Premium access.

Apple processes this information under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include, such as device model, iOS version, screenshots, or diagnostic details. We use this information only to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and maintain an appropriate support record.

Please do not send sensitive personal, health, or financial information in a support request.

6. Third-party services

Apple

Apple distributes the app, processes purchases, provides StoreKit services, and may process App Store diagnostics according to its own terms and privacy policy.

GitHub Pages

These public webpages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process standard web request information such as IP address, browser type, and request time for security and service operation. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

5M Sleep currently does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device information: remains on your device until you change it, reset it, or delete the app.
  • Purchase records: are retained by Apple according to Apple’s policies. We do not control Apple’s legally required transaction records.
  • Support emails: are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain support history, meet legal obligations, or protect the Service.

You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us. We will honor verified requests unless retention is required by law or for legitimate security and dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because the app has no user accounts and sends us no app-usage data, we generally cannot identify app data as belonging to a particular person.

See Privacy Choices for practical instructions and how to submit a request.

9. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the limited information we handle. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service, our practices, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes will be communicated when required by law.
