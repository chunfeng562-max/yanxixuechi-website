Getting started and recording your work

Where do I start a focus session?

Open the Steps tab and tap Start Focus to open the session timer. Choose a topic and an optional task in Working on, then tap Start Round 1, or the button showing your fixed round target. The four main tabs are Steps, Journal, Stats, and Settings. During an active session, Continue returns to it.

Closing the session screen with its close button returns to the Steps path; it does not stop a running session. Pause pauses the current phase. Use the separate Stop control to end the whole session.

How do I change focus time, breaks, and rounds?

Before starting, slide the timer face or turn its dial to set 1–60 minutes of focus. Tap Session cadence to choose 1–30 rounds or Unlimited and a 1–30 minute break. Tap Done to apply changes; Cancel discards them. The initial timing values are 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break; first-time setup lets you choose your own rhythm.

Focus time is fixed once a session starts. You can still change the round target while it remains editable and adjust the break length. Changing the length during a break adjusts that break. Each new phase starts when you choose it. Wait, or Stop on a system alarm, silences the alert without starting the next phase. The final focus round of a fixed-round session ends the session without another break.

What should I know about long breaks?

First-time setup includes a longer break after every four completed focus rounds. In the current build, that setting may return to ordinary short breaks after the app is reopened. Check the break length shown in your session and adjust it when needed. Contact support if the rhythm does not match the one you selected. A session that reaches its fixed round target ends without another break.

How do I end a session?

Press and hold the separate circular Stop control, marked with a square, beside the Pause or Resume control. Keep holding until the 3-second countdown finishes; release early to cancel. A tap shows the hold instruction. With VoiceOver, activate End session and confirm.

Your measured focus time is retained, including time from an unfinished round; only finished focus rounds count as completed rounds. On Session Complete, add an optional Reflection or photos and tap Save, or tap Skip to leave without adding a reflection or new photos. Skip does not remove the timing record or the steps already saved during that session.

What are topics, steps, and reflections?

A topic names the work, such as Study or Writing; the task describes what you want to do in this session. Add a Step captures text and an optional Idea, Question, or Parked label, with optional photos, while you work. Reflection is the optional note at the end of the whole session.

The timer keeps running while Add a Step is open. If a phase ends while you are writing with the app in the foreground, its alert waits until you close the editor. Background system reminders still depend on your permissions. Journal keeps the session, its steps, and its reflection together. Stats can open the sessions for the period you are viewing.

Why do Journal and Stats show sample records?

Before your first completed session, Journal and Stats can show a labeled Sample data preview so you can see how records work. Choose Hide to dismiss it, or finish your own session to switch to your records. The Steps path does not count the welcome sample as your completed work. Sample records do not overwrite your own history.

Free features and Steps Pro

What is included without Steps Pro?

Basic timing, topics and tasks, written steps and their labels, reflections, every saved session record, and the Default App Shield mode are available without Pro. One photo per step and one photo per session are free. Free choices also include Classic Tomato and LED Segment, Gentle rain, Soft bell and Chime, and Day and Week statistics within the current week.

Steps Pro adds up to nine photos per step and per session, five more available timer faces, the other ambient sounds and Wood tap, custom App Shield modes, Month statistics, and statistics for earlier weeks. Pro does not add cloud backup or synchronization.

How do I add or remove photos?

In Add a Step, Edit Step, Session Complete, or Edit Session, tap Add a photo and use the system photo picker or Camera when available. One photo per step or session is free. Adding more photos opens Steps Pro unless Pro is active; Pro allows up to nine in each step or session. The first photo can be removed and replaced without Pro.

To remove a photo from a saved record, open its editor, tap the × on that photo, then Save. This deletes the app’s copy after saving, not the original in Apple Photos. Existing photos remain viewable and removable when Pro ends, even if there are more than the current free limit. Try an image smaller than 25 MB and check free storage if an image cannot be added.

How do I buy or restore Steps Pro?

Open the Steps Pro card in Settings, or select a feature marked PRO, to see Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime options. Monthly and Yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions; Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no recurring billing. Review the price, currency, and billing period on Apple’s purchase confirmation before confirming. Product availability can vary by storefront.

To restore an eligible purchase, use Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Pro screen with the same Apple Account. If products cannot load, check your connection and try again. A purchase awaiting approval unlocks Pro after Apple confirms it. Restoring Pro does not recover deleted records or transfer session data or photos between devices.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Use Settings → Steps Pro → Manage subscription when available, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose the subscription, and cancel it. Cancellation stops future renewals; paid access normally continues until the current period ends. Deleting Steps does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime does not renew. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.

For refund requests, visit Apple’s purchase and refund support. Refund eligibility is determined through Apple’s process and applicable consumer law. Never email payment card details or your Apple Account password.

What happens when Pro ends?

Your sessions, steps, reflections, and existing photos stay on your device. You can still browse every saved session and view or remove existing photos. Without Pro, you can add or replace a photo while the step or session has room within the one-photo free limit; adding beyond that limit requires Pro.

Month and earlier-week statistics and using custom App Shield modes require Pro. Selected Pro faces and sounds return to free choices; custom modes remain saved and Default takes over. Expiration does not delete your records or cancel any separate Apple purchase.

Reminders, App Shield, and your data

Why did I not hear an alarm or reminder?

Open Settings → Alert style and choose Alarm or Notification. Follow any permission notice, then review the app’s alarm and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Notifications may be quiet in Silent mode or Focus. In-app alert sounds are configured separately under Settings → Sounds.

Steps uses authorized system alarms when selected, or notifications when that option is selected or alarm access is unavailable and notification access is allowed. Even with permission, reminders may be affected by system limitations. Test a short session with your device locked, and contact support if reminders do not arrive. Do not rely on Steps for safety-critical reminders.

How do I set up App Shield?

Open Settings → App Shield, or tap the App Shield label on the session timer. Complete Apple’s Screen Time authorization, then choose apps, categories, or websites in the system picker. Use Edit on Default to change its selection. Choosing a mode selects it and turns blocking on; the App Shield switch turns blocking on or off. Default is free; adding and using your own named modes requires Steps Pro.

Selected items are blocked during a running focus phase. The app releases its restrictions when you pause, take a break, or end the session. Screen Time authorization and actual blocking require a physical device; other apps’ or parental controls’ restrictions are separate.

What if an app stays blocked?

Reopen Steps, pause or end the current session, then turn off App Shield. If the restriction remains, revoke Steps’ Screen Time access in iOS Settings. If Steps was force-quit or terminated during a short focus round, the fallback release can happen later than the timer deadline; reopening Steps lets it reconcile the timer and restrictions. Contact support if the issue continues.

Where are my records, and why can daily totals differ?

Records and photos are stored on your device. Steps has no app account or app-operated cloud synchronization, so they do not automatically sync between devices. Apple device backups may include them depending on your settings. Restore Purchases restores eligible Pro access, not your journal.

Journal groups finished sessions by their end date. Stats divides reliable recorded time across the dates when it occurred and uses finished phases; an active phase may not yet appear in the totals. A session crossing midnight can therefore have different daily totals in Journal and Stats. Keep independent copies of important original photos and notes.

How do I edit or delete a session, step, or photo?

Open Journal, find the session, open its … menu and choose Edit. In Edit Session, use Delete this session and confirm to remove the session, its steps, reflection, photos, and its contribution to Stats.

To edit or delete one step, open that session’s Edit view, tap its footprint and then the step to open Edit Step; use Delete Step and confirm. To remove only a photo, tap its × in the corresponding editor and Save. Deleting a step does not delete the whole session or its timing totals. See Your data & privacy choices for permission, backup, and deletion details.

Why are Live Activities not visible?

On iPhone, check Settings → Live Activities in Steps and the corresponding iOS setting for the app. Availability depends on the device, permissions, and whether a session is active. Dynamic Island also requires a compatible iPhone. The Live Activities setting is not offered in the iPad app.
