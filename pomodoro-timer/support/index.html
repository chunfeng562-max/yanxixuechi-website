Common questions

How do I change focus and break durations?

Open Settings → Timer to adjust Focus duration, Short break, and Long breaks. Finish the current session if these controls are unavailable. Each new phase starts when you choose it; dismissing an alert does not automatically start the next phase.

Why did I not hear an alarm or reminder?

Check Settings → Timer alarms and Alarm access in the app. Review the app’s alarm and notification permissions in iOS Settings, as well as sound volume and Focus settings. The app uses system alarms where available and notifications as a fallback. Test a short session with your device locked before relying on reminders.

How do I set up app blocking?

Open Settings → Focus Shield, choose Allow access, and complete Apple’s Screen Time authorization. Use Choose to select apps or categories, enable App blocking, and tap Done. The app blocks selected items during a running focus phase and releases them when you pause, take a break, or end the session. Screen Time access must be tested on a real device.

What if an app stays blocked?

Reopen this app, pause or end the current session, and turn off App blocking in Settings → Focus Shield. If the restriction remains, revoke this app’s Screen Time access in iOS Settings. If another app or parental control also applies restrictions, manage that separately. Email us if you need help.

Where are my session records and photos?

They are stored on your device. The current app has no account or app-operated cloud synchronization, so records do not automatically sync between devices. Apple device backups may include the data depending on your settings.

How do I delete my data?

Open a session’s editing view and choose Delete session record, or remove a photo and save the changes. For all local data, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App retains data. Follow the detailed steps on Your data & privacy choices.

Why can I not attach a photo?

Select an image using the photo picker or allow Camera access to take a picture. Try a JPEG or PNG smaller than 25 MB and check that the device has free storage. You can keep using the timer without adding photos.

Why are Live Activities not visible?

Check Settings → Live Activities in the app and the corresponding iOS setting for the app. Availability depends on your device, iOS version, permissions, and whether a timer is active.

How can I ask about an App Store charge?

The current app build does not implement in-app purchases or subscriptions. If Apple shows a charge you do not recognize, check the app and seller on your receipt. For Apple-billed purchases, visit Apple’s purchase and refund support. Never email payment card details.

About this app

Pomodoro Timer: Study & Focus is a productivity timer for focused work, study sessions, and breaks. It may appear as Steps on your device. The current app requires iOS 26 or later or iPadOS 26 or later.
