Common questions

How do I change focus time, breaks, and rounds?

On Timer, slide the timer face or turn its dial before starting to set 1–60 minutes of focus. Tap the session progress tray to open Session, choose 1–30 rounds or Unlimited, and set a 1–30 minute break. Defaults are 25 minutes of focus, a 5-minute break, and Unlimited rounds. Focus time is fixed once a session starts. You can still change the round target and break length; changing the length during a break adjusts that break. The current app does not offer a separate long-break schedule.

Each new phase starts when you choose it. Wait, or Stop on a system alarm, silences the alert without starting the next phase. The final focus round of a fixed-round session ends the session without another break.

How do I end a session?

Press and hold the main timer button until the 3-second countdown finishes. Release early to cancel. Where an End session action is shown, use it and confirm. Your measured focus time is saved, including an unfinished round; only finished focus rounds count toward completed rounds. Skip on the result page skips the optional note, tags, and photo, not the timing record.

Why did I not hear an alarm or reminder?

Open Settings → Alert style and choose Alarm or Notification. Follow any permission notice, then review the app’s alarm and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Notifications may be quiet in Silent mode or Focus. In-app alert sounds are configured separately under Settings → Sounds. The app uses authorized system alarms when selected, with a notification fallback when available. Test a short session with your device locked before relying on reminders.

How do I set up App Shield?

Open Settings → App Shield or tap App Shield on Timer. Complete Apple’s Screen Time authorization when requested, then choose the apps to block. Use Edit on the Default mode to change its apps, categories, or websites; select a mode to turn blocking on. The App Shield switch on Timer turns blocking on or off. Default is available without Pro; adding and using custom modes requires Steps Pro.

Selected items are blocked during a running focus phase and released when you pause, take a break, or end the session. Screen Time authorization requires a physical device.

What if an app stays blocked?

Reopen Steps, pause or end the current session, and turn off the App Shield switch on Timer. If the restriction remains, revoke Steps’ Screen Time access in iOS Settings. If Steps was force-quit during a short focus round, the system’s fallback release may happen later than the timer deadline; reopening Steps lets it reconcile the timer and restrictions. Other apps or parental controls may also apply restrictions and must be managed separately. Contact support if the issue continues.

What is included without Steps Pro?

Basic timing, notes, tags, every saved session record, and the Default App Shield mode are available without Pro. You also get Classic Tomato and LED Segment, Gentle rain, Soft bell and Chime, and Day and Week statistics within the current week. Steps Pro adds five more faces, the other ambient sounds and Wood tap, custom App Shield modes, Month and earlier-week statistics, and adding or replacing session photos.

How do I buy or restore Steps Pro?

Open Settings → Steps Pro, or select a feature marked PRO, to see Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime options. Monthly and Yearly renew automatically; Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Apple’s purchase sheet confirms the amount and billing period before you buy.

To restore an eligible purchase, use Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Pro screen with the same Apple Account. If products cannot load, check your connection and try again. A purchase awaiting approval unlocks Pro after Apple confirms it. Restoring Pro does not transfer session records or photos between devices.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Use Settings → Steps Pro → Manage subscription when available, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel there to stop future renewals. Deleting Steps does not cancel your subscription. Lifetime does not renew. For refund requests, visit Apple’s purchase and refund support. Never email payment card details or your Apple Account password.

What happens to my records when Pro ends?

Your session records, notes, tags, and existing photos stay on your device. You can still view all records and view or remove existing photos. Adding or replacing photos, custom App Shield modes, Month statistics, and statistics before the current week require Pro. Selected Pro faces and sounds return to free choices; custom modes remain saved and Default takes over.

Where are my session records and photos?

They are stored on your device. Steps has no app account or app-operated cloud synchronization, so records do not automatically sync between devices. Apple device backups may include the data depending on your settings. Steps groups finished sessions by their end date; Stats divides recorded time across the dates when it occurred, so a session crossing midnight can produce different daily totals.

How do I delete my data?

In Steps, tap Edit on a session and choose Delete session record, or open the photo’s options, choose Remove Photo, and save. Deleting a session also removes its contribution to Stats. For all local data, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App retains data. See Your data & privacy choices.

Why can I not attach a photo?

Adding or replacing a session photo requires Steps Pro. With Pro active, select a picture using the system photo picker or allow Camera access to take one. Try a JPEG or PNG smaller than 25 MB and check free storage. The app stores one resized photo per session. Viewing or removing a photo already saved does not require Pro.

Why are Live Activities not visible?

On iPhone, check Settings → Live Activities in Steps and the corresponding iOS setting for the app. Availability depends on the device, permissions, and whether a session is active. Dynamic Island also requires a compatible iPhone. The Live Activities setting is not offered in the iPad app.
