POMODORO TIMER: STUDY & FOCUS

Privacy Policy

Your focus stays yours.

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026

At a glance

The current app works without an account. Timer records, notes, photos, preferences, and app-blocking selections are stored on your device. We do not receive those records through the app, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Who is responsible

Nanjing Yanxi Xuechi Energy Development Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”) provides Pomodoro Timer: Study & Focus, also identified as Steps within the current app. This policy covers the app and its support and legal pages. Contact us at chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com or visit yanxixuechi-energy.com.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Timer and session data: focus and break durations, timestamps, completed sessions, pauses, and statistics are used to operate the timer and show your history.
  • Notes and photos: text you add, photos you select through Apple’s photo picker, and pictures you choose to take are attached to session records. Selected images are resized and saved as local files.
  • Preferences: appearance, sounds, reminders, and other settings are stored locally.
  • App Shield: Apple’s Family Controls picker provides privacy-preserving selection tokens for apps, categories, and web domains you choose. The app stores these selections locally and uses Managed Settings and Device Activity to apply and release restrictions during focus sessions. We do not receive your selections or browsing history.

We do not operate an account system, cloud synchronization service, or server that receives your timer records, notes, or photos. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone access, or an advertising identifier.

Purchases and Pro access

Apple processes Steps Pro purchases and keeps purchase history under its own policies. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to read verified purchase information on your device, including the product purchased, subscription expiration, and whether a purchase has been revoked. It uses that information to unlock Pro, restore purchases, and respond to renewals, expiration, or refunds. The last known Pro plan is cached in your device’s preferences.

The app does not send this purchase information to a server operated by us or to a third-party analytics provider. We do not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password. Apple may provide sales, financial, and aggregate app reports through its developer services under its own policies. If you send us a receipt or purchase information for support, we handle it as support correspondence.

3. Permissions and system features

  • Screen Time / Family Controls: optional permission for the apps and categories you decide to block. The timer works without this permission. Screen Time information is not used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • Camera and photo picker: used only when you choose to add a session photo. The system picker lets you select individual photos without granting access to your entire library.
  • Alarms and notifications: used to alert you when a focus round or break ends, subject to your choices and iOS settings.
  • Live Activities: may display timer status on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, where someone viewing your device may see it.

You can decline optional permissions and manage them in iOS Settings. See Your data & privacy choices for practical steps.

4. Support messages and website visits

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, Screen Time passcodes, or unnecessary sensitive information. You can contact us without sending your session history.

These static pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or contact forms. The hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, and timestamps to deliver and secure the website. Hosting logs are separate from the app’s on-device session data. See Netlify’s privacy policy.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Local app content is not sent to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Email and hosting providers process support correspondence and website requests as needed to deliver those services. Where a provider processes personal information on our behalf, we require protection consistent with this policy and applicable law, and limit its use to the relevant service. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect people’s rights and security.

Apple operates the App Store, system permissions, device backups, and optional diagnostics under its own settings and policies. Depending on your Apple settings, Apple may provide developers with crash reports or aggregated app information. The app itself does not include a separate telemetry uploader. Read Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Retention, deletion, and backups

Local records remain until you delete them or delete the app. Deleting a session removes its note, associated photo, and session data from the app’s statistics. Removing a photo from a session removes the app’s copy; it does not delete the original from your photo library. Offloading an app may retain its data; use Delete App to remove the local app data.

Your device or iCloud backup may contain app data according to your Apple backup settings. Deleting local data does not necessarily remove older backups; manage those separately through Apple. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete records stored only on your device. Deleting local app data does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase purchase history maintained by Apple. A valid Pro purchase may be restored through the same Apple Account, but this does not restore deleted local records or photos. See Your data & privacy choices for subscription and purchase controls.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request, maintain necessary support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. Hosting providers retain technical logs under their applicable policies and service settings.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information we hold, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Email chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com to exercise these rights. We may request proportionate information to verify the request. You may also contact your local data protection authority. Withdrawing permission does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Where a legal basis is required, we process support messages to provide requested assistance, website request information for legitimate interests in secure service delivery, and information needed to satisfy legal obligations. Optional device features use the permissions you grant. Service providers may process information outside your country; applicable legal safeguards are required where relevant.

8. Children and security

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and does not require identifying information or a child account. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle. Local data also depends on your device security and backup settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change, and provide additional notice or request consent when required. Privacy questions can be sent to chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com.
