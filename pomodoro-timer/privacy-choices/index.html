STUDY TIMER: STEPS

Your Data & Privacy Choices

Control what you keep.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Delete a session, step, or photo

A whole session

  1. Open Journal and find the session. Open its … menu and choose Edit.
  2. In Edit Session, tap Delete this session and confirm.

This removes the session’s task and reflection, its steps and their labels, all photos stored for that session and its steps, and its timing records from Stats. It does not remove originals from Apple Photos. Deleting a session does not necessarily remove a topic from the list offered for future sessions.

One step or its contents

From Edit Session, tap a step’s footprint to open the session’s steps, then tap the step to open Edit Step. Change its text or label and Save, or tap Delete Step and confirm. Deleting a step removes its text, label, and associated app-stored photos; it does not delete the session, its reflection, or its timing totals.

Only a photo or reflection

Open Edit Step for a step photo or Edit Session for a session photo. Tap the × on each photo you want to remove, then Save. Removing a saved photo does not require Pro and does not delete the original in your photo library. To clear a session reflection, erase the Reflection text in Edit Session and Save. Changes to a record apply when you save the corresponding editor.

Topics and drafts

Use Remove in the topic chooser to stop offering a topic for future sessions; existing sessions keep their recorded topic. For a step you have not saved, Cancel discards the draft. Saved records can be deleted through the controls above.

Remove all local app data

End any active session and turn off App Shield first. In iOS Settings, open General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage), select Steps, and choose Delete App. Offload App retains data and is not the same as deleting it. This removes the app’s local records and preferences; review your iCloud or computer backups separately if you also want to remove saved backup copies. There is no Steps app account to close.

We do not operate a server that receives your journal, so we cannot retrieve, export, or remotely delete records stored only on your device. Keep separate copies of anything important before deleting it. Restore Purchases does not restore deleted records or photos.

Manage App Shield and other permissions

Use the App Shield switch on the session timer to turn blocking off. To change a selection, open Settings → App Shield, tap Edit on a mode, then Choose or Change to select apps, categories, or websites. To withdraw Screen Time access, open iOS Settings → Screen Time and find Apps with Screen Time Access, then disable access for Steps. System labels may vary.

If an app remains blocked, reopen Steps, pause or end the session, and turn off App Shield. Revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings if needed. A fallback release after the app was terminated during a short round can be later than the timer deadline. See Support if the problem continues.

In iOS Settings, open Apps → Steps to review Camera, Alarms, Notifications, and Live Activities where available. Notifications may also be under Settings → Notifications. The photo picker gives access only to the pictures you select; stop selecting new photos if you do not want to attach them. Withdrawing a permission does not automatically erase records or photos already saved in the app.

Manage subscriptions and purchases

Deleting the app, a photo, or a session does not cancel a subscription or remove purchase history maintained by Apple. To stop renewals, use Settings → Steps Pro → Manage subscription when available, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no renewal to cancel. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Restore Purchases on the Pro screen restores eligible Pro access through the Apple Account used to buy it, not your journal or deleted content. Pro expiration does not delete existing local records or photos. One photo per step and per session remains free; adding beyond that limit requires Pro, up to nine. Existing photos remain viewable and removable. Contact Apple for Apple Account and purchase-history controls; refund requests go through Apple’s purchase and refund support.

Request access or deletion of support information

Email chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com with the subject “Steps privacy request”. Say whether you want access, correction, or deletion of information you previously sent us. We need only enough information to locate your correspondence and verify the request. Do not send passwords, Screen Time passcodes, or identification documents unless a necessary verification method has been agreed with you.

These requests concern information we hold, such as support correspondence; local journal content is managed on your device. For retention, backup copies, website request information, and your rights, read our Privacy Policy.
