POMODORO TIMER: STUDY & FOCUS

Your Data & Privacy Choices

Control what you keep.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Delete a session, note, or photo

  1. Open the Steps timeline in the app and tap Edit on the session record.
  2. Open its editing view. Use Delete session record and confirm to remove its note, associated photo, and focus records from Stats.
  3. To keep the session but remove its picture, open the photo’s options, choose Remove Photo, and save your changes. Removing an existing photo does not require Pro. An original photo in Apple Photos is not removed.

Remove all local app data

Stop any active session and turn off the App Shield switch on Timer first. In iOS Settings, open General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage), select the app, and choose Delete App. Offload App retains data and is not the same as deleting it. Check your iCloud or computer backups separately if you also want to remove saved backup copies. There is no app account to close.

Manage App Shield

Use the App Shield switch on Timer to turn blocking off. To change the selection, open Settings → App Shield, tap Edit on a mode, and use Choose or Change to select apps, categories, or websites. To withdraw Screen Time access, open iOS Settings → Screen Time and look for Apps with Screen Time Access, then disable access for Steps. Labels can vary by iOS version.

When an app remains blocked unexpectedly, reopen Steps, pause or end the active session, and turn off App Shield on Timer. If needed, revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings. Contact support if the issue continues.

Manage subscriptions and purchases

Deleting the app, a photo, or a session does not cancel a subscription or remove purchase history maintained by Apple. To stop renewals, use Settings → Steps Pro → Manage subscription when available, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and has no renewal to cancel. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Restore Purchases on the Pro screen can restore eligible Pro access through the Apple Account used to buy it. It does not recover deleted records or photos. Pro expiration does not delete existing local records or photos. Contact Apple for Apple Account and purchase-history controls; refund requests are handled through Apple’s purchase and refund support.

Change other permissions

In iOS Settings, open Apps → Steps (or the app’s displayed name) to review Camera, Alarms, Notifications, and Live Activities where available. Notification settings may also appear under Settings → Notifications. You can simply stop selecting photos if you do not want to attach new images.

Request access or deletion of support information

Email chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com with the subject “Pomodoro privacy request”. Tell us whether you want access, correction, or deletion of information you previously sent us. We only need enough information to find your correspondence and verify the request. Please do not send passwords, passcodes, or identification documents unless a necessary verification method has been agreed with you.

We cannot access records stored only on your device. For details about retention, backup copies, hosting logs, and your rights, read our Privacy Policy.
