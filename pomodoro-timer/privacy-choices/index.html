POMODORO TIMER: FOCUS & STUDY

Your Data & Privacy Choices

Control what you keep.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Delete a session, note, or photo

  1. Open the Steps timeline in the app and select the session record.
  2. Open its editing view. Use Delete session record and confirm to remove its note, associated photo, and focus records from Stats.
  3. To keep the session but remove its picture, choose Remove photo and save your changes. An original photo in Apple Photos is not removed.

Remove all local app data

Stop any active session and turn off App blocking first. In iOS Settings, open General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage), select the app, and choose Delete App. Offload App retains data and is not the same as deleting it. Check your iCloud or computer backups separately if you also want to remove saved backup copies. There is no app account to close.

Manage Focus Shield

In the app, open Settings → Focus Shield and turn off App blocking. Use Choose or Change to update the selection of apps and categories. To withdraw Screen Time access, open iOS Settings → Screen Time and look for Apps with Screen Time Access, then disable access for this app. Labels can vary by iOS version.

When an app remains blocked unexpectedly, reopen the timer app, pause or end the active session, and turn off App blocking. If needed, revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings. Contact support if the issue continues.

Change other permissions

In iOS Settings, open Apps → Steps (or the app’s displayed name) to review Camera, Alarms, Notifications, and Live Activities where available. Notification settings may also appear under Settings → Notifications. You can simply stop selecting photos if you do not want to attach new images.

Request access or deletion of support information

Email chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com with the subject “Pomodoro privacy request”. Tell us whether you want access, correction, or deletion of information you previously sent us. We only need enough information to find your correspondence and verify the request. Please do not send passwords, passcodes, or identification documents unless a necessary verification method has been agreed with you.

We cannot access records stored only on your device. For details about retention, backup copies, hosting logs, and your rights, read our Privacy Policy.
