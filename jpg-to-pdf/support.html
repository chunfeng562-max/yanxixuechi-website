Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos, scans, and PDFs processed?
The app processes photos, scans, edits, and PDF creation on your device. It does not upload those files to our servers. Generated PDFs remain in the app’s local Files list until you delete them or remove the app; exported copies remain at the destinations you chose.
Why can’t I import a photo?
Make sure the item is a supported still image and try exporting it as JPEG or HEIC from Photos. The app safely downsizes very large camera images to reduce memory use. If the issue continues, restart the app and select the image again.
Why can’t I use the camera or scanner?
Open iOS Settings, find JPG to PDF, and allow Camera access. Confirm that your device has an available camera and enough free storage. The scanner is provided by supported Apple system frameworks and may not be available on every device configuration.
How do I create a PDF?
Tap Create to select photos or Scan to scan pages. Arrange or edit the pages, choose Letter, A4, or Original page size and an output quality, enter a file name, then tap Create PDF. Preview the result and use Share PDF to export it.
Why are there margins around my images?
Letter and A4 output fit each image inside a standard page with a small white margin. Select Original when you want each PDF page to follow the processed image dimensions.
What is included for free?
You can create PDFs of up to 10 pages for free. Lifetime Pro unlocks larger batches and unlimited conversions supported by the app. The price and exact offer shown by the App Store before purchase control.
Is Lifetime Pro a subscription?
No. Lifetime Pro is a one-time in-app purchase and does not automatically renew.
How do I restore Lifetime Pro?
Open JPG to PDF, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase and make sure the device can connect to the App Store.
How do I request a refund?
Apple handles App Store payments and refund decisions. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and select the purchase. We cannot issue App Store refunds directly.
How do I delete a PDF or all app data?
Delete an individual PDF from the app’s Files screen. To remove all app-local PDFs, metadata, and preferences, delete the app from your device. Files you previously exported must be deleted separately from their destination. See Privacy Choices for details.
Does JPG to PDF have an account?
No. The app does not require or provide an account. There is no cloud account or developer-hosted document library to delete.
