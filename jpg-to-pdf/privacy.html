1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the JPG to PDF mobile application and its support and legal webpages (collectively, the “Service”). The Service is provided by Nanjing Yanxi Xuechi Energy Development Co., Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

2. App data and on-device processing

Short version: The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not transmit your photos, scans, generated PDFs, or app-usage data to us.

The app processes images you choose, photographs you take, and pages you scan to create and edit PDF documents. This processing—including document-edge detection, cropping, rotation, image enhancement, compression, PDF creation, previewing, and file management—occurs on your device.

We do not collect through the app your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, scans, PDF content, advertising identifier, or usage history. We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use app information to track you across apps or websites.

3. Photos, camera, and system permissions

You choose when to provide the app with images. The iOS Photos picker lets you select specific images. Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo or scan a document. Images made available to the app are used to provide the PDF features you request and are not uploaded to us.

You can review or change camera and photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Revoking access may prevent the related import, camera, or scanning feature from working.

4. Information stored on your device

The app stores generated PDF files and limited file metadata, such as file name, page count, size, and creation date, in the app’s local storage. It may also store interface and purchase-entitlement state needed to operate the app.

Your device operating system and your Apple backup settings may include app data in an encrypted device or iCloud backup. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by our servers.

When you use the iOS share sheet to export a PDF, the destination app or service you select processes the shared file under its own privacy practices.

5. Apple and in-app purchases

JPG to PDF may offer an optional one-time Lifetime Pro in-app purchase. Apple processes payment through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your full Apple Account credentials or payment-card details. The app receives limited product and transaction information from Apple, such as product identifier and verified entitlement status, only as needed to display the offer, complete or restore a purchase, and unlock Pro features.

Apple processes this information under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to include, such as device model, iOS version, app version, screenshots, or diagnostic details. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and maintain an appropriate support record.

Please do not send original photos, scans, PDFs, or other sensitive personal, financial, health, or identity information unless it is necessary and you understand the risks.

7. Website and third-party services

Website hosting

These public webpages are hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process standard web request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time for security and service operation. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

We do not place advertising or analytics cookies on these pages. Links to Apple, email providers, or other external services take you to services governed by their own privacy terms.

Apple

Apple distributes the app, processes purchases, provides StoreKit and Photos or camera system interfaces, and may process App Store diagnostics according to Apple’s policies and your settings.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Photos and scans: are held in app memory as needed to edit and create a PDF; they are not retained by us.
  • Generated PDFs and metadata: remain in the app’s local storage until you delete individual files or remove the app. Copies you exported remain wherever you saved or shared them.
  • Purchase records: are retained by Apple according to Apple’s policies. We do not control Apple’s legally required transaction records.
  • Support emails: are retained only as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, keep support history, meet legal obligations, or protect the Service.

See Privacy Choices for deletion steps and request instructions.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because the app has no accounts and does not send us your app data, we generally cannot identify app data as belonging to a particular person.

You may manage local data and permissions on your device. You may also request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where required by law or reasonably needed for security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

10. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

11. Security

On-device PDF files receive the protections provided by iOS and your device security settings. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited support information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Protect your device and use care when sharing PDFs that contain sensitive content.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service, our practices, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. We will provide additional notice where required by law.
