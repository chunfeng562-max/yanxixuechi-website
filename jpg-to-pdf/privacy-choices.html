Privacy

Your privacy choices

Last updated: August 13, 2026

JPG to PDF has no user accounts and does not upload your photos, scans, generated PDFs, or app-usage data to us. Most privacy choices are controlled directly on your device.

Delete a generated PDF

Open the Files tab in JPG to PDF and use the delete control or swipe action for the PDF. This removes the file from the app’s local storage. Copies already shared or exported must be deleted separately from each destination.

Delete all app-local data

Delete JPG to PDF from your iPhone or iPad to remove its locally stored PDF files, file metadata, and preferences from that device. If you restore your device from an Apple backup, backed-up app data may be restored according to your Apple settings. App Store purchase records remain with Apple as required for purchase history and restoration.

Control camera and photo access

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, then review Camera or Photos. You can also find JPG to PDF in iOS Settings and adjust the permissions shown there. Revoking access may disable related features.

Delete support communications

If you emailed us, you may ask us to delete the support correspondence associated with your email address. Send the request from the same address to chun.feng@yanxixuechi-energy.com with the subject “JPG to PDF Privacy Request.” We may need to verify the request and may retain limited records where required by law or reasonably necessary for security or dispute resolution.

Access, correction, and other rights

Depending on your location, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information we hold about you, and you may have additional rights. Because the app sends us no app data and creates no account, we generally hold no app information that can be linked to you. Contact us about information you voluntarily sent in a support request.

No sale, targeted advertising, or tracking opt-out needed

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, do not serve targeted ads, and do not track you across third-party apps or websites. The app therefore does not provide a separate “Do Not Sell or Share” switch.
