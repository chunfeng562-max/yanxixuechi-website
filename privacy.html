Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Math Alarm handles information when you use the iOS app or contact support.

Effective date: August 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 10, 2026

Plain-language summary: Math Alarm does not require an account and does not transmit your alarm schedules, app settings, challenge results, or usage statistics to us. The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. App data is stored locally on your device.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Math Alarm mobile application with bundle identifier com.yanxixuechi-energy.Mathe-Alarm-Clock (the “App”), the related legal and support pages, and support communications sent to us. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer of Math Alarm.

2. Information handled by the App

The current version of Math Alarm does not collect personal data from the App or transmit App usage data to servers operated by us. The following information may be created and processed locally on your device so the App can function:

Information Purpose Storage
Alarm schedules and settings To schedule and display alarms, including time, recurrence, label, sound, and math challenge preferences. On your device.
App preferences To remember settings such as theme, default challenge options, gradual wake preferences, and onboarding status. On your device.
Wake-up and habit statistics To show your progress and history within the App. On your device.
Generated audio resources To prepare and play selected alarm sounds and white noise. In the App’s local storage on your device.

We do not use this locally stored information for advertising, tracking, profiling, or sale. The App does not request your name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

3. Device permissions and Apple system services

Math Alarm uses Apple-provided system technologies to deliver its features. Depending on your device and operating system version, the App may ask for or rely on:

  • Alarms and notifications: to schedule and present wake-up alerts, actions, sounds, and badges.
  • Live Activities: to show relevant alarm status on supported Lock Screens and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Background audio: to continue permitted alarm sound or white-noise playback when the App is not in the foreground.

You can manage applicable permissions in iOS Settings. Apple may process technical information when providing App Store, notification, alarm, Live Activity, operating-system, or device services. That processing is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policies; we do not receive personal data from Apple merely because the App uses these on-device services.

4. Analytics, advertising, tracking, and third parties

The current version of the App does not include third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics, data brokers, social tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent personal information.

If future versions introduce a service that collects or transmits data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before or when the change becomes effective, and request consent where required.

5. Information you choose to send to support

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, device model, iOS version, and App version. We use support communications only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App and users, and comply with legal obligations.

Please do not send passwords, financial information, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive personal data in a support request.

6. Retention and deletion

Information stored by the App

Local App information remains on your device until you edit or delete it in the App, reset applicable settings, or uninstall the App. Because we do not receive this local information, we cannot remotely access, export, or delete it for you.

Support communications

We retain support emails only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, maintain support records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of a support conversation by emailing us. We may retain limited information when required by law or when necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

See Privacy Choices for step-by-step controls.

7. Security

Math Alarm minimizes data handling by keeping its functional data on your device and relying on iOS application sandboxing and device security controls. No method of electronic storage or communication is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children, and the current version does not transmit personal data to us. If a child contacts support and a parent or guardian believes we received personal information, the parent or guardian may contact us to request deletion.

9. International users

The App’s functional data remains on the user’s device. If you email us, your message may be processed by email and network providers in countries other than your own. Those providers handle information under their own terms and legal obligations.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing. Since we do not maintain user accounts or receive the App’s local data, most App data choices are exercised directly on your device. For support communications, contact us using the details below.

We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable requirements. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how the App handles personal information, we will provide additional notice where required.
